# 📚 Web Amarakosha 

A digital version of the **Amarakosha**, the ancient Sanskrit thesaurus compiled by **Amarasimha**. This project aims to make the Amarakosha accessible, searchable, and user-friendly through a modern web interface.

## 🌟 About Amarakosha

The **Amarakosha** (अमरकोशः) is one of the oldest and most authoritative Sanskrit lexicons (dictionary/thesaurus), composed by the ancient Indian scholar **Amarasimha**, who is believed to have lived around the **5th to 7th century CE**.

It is unique because it presents Sanskrit words grouped thematically, not alphabetically, making it easier to learn vocabulary in context. The text is entirely in verse form, aiding memorization.

## 🧠 Structure of Amarakosha

The Amarakosha is traditionally divided into **three Kandas** (sections), also known as **Trikāṇḍī**:

1. ### **Prathama Kanda** 
   - Contains names and synonyms of gods, heavens, celestial beings, time, and related concepts.
2. ### **Dwiteeya Kanda** (Earth and Geography)
   - Includes words related to the Earth, mountains, rivers, cities, plants, animals, humans, and more.
3. ### **Triteeya Kanda** (General Vocabulary)
   - Covers general nouns, adjectives, verbs, and abstract terms across various domains.

Together, these sections contain over **10,000 words and synonyms**, organized metrically for ease of memorization.

## 🧩 What I Have Done

While the Amarakosha text and even some commentaries are already available online for free, most of them present only the **textual data**—either in transliterated form or with limited annotations.

However, **none of them offer a visual or interactive experience**, especially for learning:

- What a word actually looks like (through images)
- Its real-world reference (for animals, birds, etc.)
- A clear display of **all its synonyms** listed in the kosha

### 🎯 My Contribution:

- Focused on the **Simhādīvarga** (सिंहादिवर्गः) section from the **second Kanda (Bhūvargādikāṇḍa)**.
- This section includes names and synonyms of **animals and birds**.
- For each entry, I’ve added:
  - 📷 **Images** representing the word (e.g., lion, elephant, eagle)
  - 🧾 **All synonyms** listed under that word in the verse
  - ✍️ **Clean layout** to visualize the verse and meaning more intuitively

This is especially useful for:
- Students of Sanskrit
- Visual learners
- Anyone curious about classical Indian zoology and its vocabulary

This project demonstrates how **traditional Sanskrit knowledge** can be presented in a **modern, digital, and visual format**.
